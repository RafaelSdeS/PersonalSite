Current Work

Undergrad Research — UFRGS ↗

Undergraduate research under Professor Mateus Grellert on hardware-aware neural networks for Winograd convolution. On the software side, I study how restricting CNN kernels to 2×2/3×3 affects accuracy, efficiency and INT8 quantization; on the hardware side, I evaluate FPGA Winograd accelerators built by a labmate on real networks.

StatusOngoing · since 2026
RoleUndergraduate researcher

How I contribute: I run the kernel-size study end to end (152 runs in the main study, plus robustness and hybrid rounds on Tiny ImageNet and PASCAL VOC). On the FPGA side, I evaluate Verilog Winograd accelerators (F(2,3), F(4,3), F(6,3), INT8) on an AMD Alveo U200: cycle counts from netlist simulation, Fmax and power from Vivado implementation, and accuracy under the accelerator’s arithmetic.

What I’m learning: an arithmetic gain is a ceiling, not throughput — bigger Winograd tiles need wider data, more logic and harder timing closure, and fixed-point scales can wipe out accuracy; hardware numbers are only trustworthy when checked against a software reference; and power and Fmax have to be measured, not estimated.

Python Deep Learning FPGA · Vivado Quantization

Quantitative Finance ↗

Machine-learning research for portfolio management on the Brazilian equity market (B3): a point-in-time data pipeline and a LightGBM-based portfolio-construction study under walk-forward backtesting, now expanding to US equities.

StatusOngoing · since 2025
RoleSolo project

How I contribute: I built the whole pipeline: prices, CVM fundamentals with their real filing dates, delisted companies to avoid survivorship bias, and macro data; then a LightGBM alpha model, a risk model and a cost-aware optimizer. For the US expansion I’m adding SEC EDGAR fundamentals and FRED macro data, validated on the top 500 stocks and scaling to ~10,000 tickers.

What I’m learning: a configuration picked from many backtests means little until it is corrected for multiple testing (a deflated-Sharpe check showed me that); change one variable per experiment and don’t add a layer until the one below beats its baseline; and survivorship bias and filing dates matter as much as the model.

Python LightGBM Walk-Forward Validation Portfolio Optimization

StatusHackathon · 2026
ContributionAnalysis + forecasting

How I contributed: one of the two main developers (67 commits).
