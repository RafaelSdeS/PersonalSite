Current Work

Undergrad Research

Conducting undergraduate research under Professor Mateus Grellert at UFRGS on hardware-aware neural network optimization. The work restricts convolutional kernel sizes (2×2, 3×3) to target the Winograd convolution algorithm, evaluated on VOC detection and segmentation — benchmarking computational cost against the accuracy trade-off to design architectures better suited for hardware accelerators.

Python C++ CUDA

Quantitative Finance

Developing ML systems for portfolio management on the Brazilian Equity Market (B3), systematically testing strategies across the top 50 most liquid B3 stocks. Building an RL-based EIIE (Ensemble of Identical Independent Evaluators) portfolio agent, with heavy emphasis on feature engineering and walk-forward validation.

Python Reinforcement Learning Walk-Forward Validation
